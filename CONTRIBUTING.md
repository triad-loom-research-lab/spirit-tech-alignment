# Contributing to Spirit-Tech Alignment

Spirit-Tech Alignment is an open research project.

Contributions are welcome from researchers, engineers, philosophers, ethicists, theologians, security practitioners, human-rights scholars, social scientists, and others who can help test, challenge, refine, or falsify the framework.

The goal is not agreement.

The goal is rigorous examination.

---

## Ways to Contribute

Useful contributions include:

- identifying hidden assumptions
- challenging candidate moral primitives
- proposing counterexamples
- identifying cultural limitations
- testing principle conflicts
- designing adversarial scenarios
- improving benchmark design
- proposing alternative formalizations
- identifying simpler competing approaches
- reviewing technical architecture
- identifying security or governance failure modes
- replicating experiments
- reporting negative results
- improving documentation

---

## Research Discipline

Contributors should distinguish clearly among:

- theological claims
- philosophical claims
- normative claims
- engineering hypotheses
- empirical findings
- experimental results

Conceptual or moral claims should not be presented as empirical facts.

Experimental claims should include enough detail to support replication where practical.

---

## Candidate Moral Primitives

Candidate primitives are provisional.

A proposed primitive should be open to:

- revision
- narrowing
- expansion
- conflict analysis
- adversarial testing
- rejection

A primitive should not be retained merely because it sounds morally desirable.

---

## Benchmarks

Benchmark contributions should clearly document:

- research question
- scenario
- optimization pressure
- relevant principle
- baseline conditions
- expected behavior
- pass criteria
- failure criteria
- critical failure conditions
- falsification conditions

Where possible, benchmark designs should compare the full architecture against simpler baselines.

---

## Negative Results

Negative results are welcome.

If a primitive cannot be operationalized reliably, that is useful evidence.

If a simpler safeguard performs as well as the integrated framework, that is useful evidence.

If a proposed architecture component adds complexity without measurable benefit, that is useful evidence.

Spirit-Tech Alignment should improve through criticism rather than avoid it.

---

## Discussion and Review

For early-stage discussion, contributors may open a GitHub Issue describing:

- the question or concern
- the relevant document or benchmark
- the proposed change or criticism
- supporting evidence or reasoning

Technical contributions may be submitted through pull requests.

---

## Current Project Status

Spirit-Tech Alignment is currently an early-stage research framework.

The repository should not be interpreted as providing:

- production-level safety guarantees
- universal moral definitions
- validated cross-cultural standards
- completed empirical proof
- a finalized governance system

---

## Guiding Principle

> **Make the claim explicit enough that someone else can challenge it.**
